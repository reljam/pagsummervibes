Pag: Your Summer Escape

Wander through the enchanting cobblestone streets of Pag, a town steeped in history that dates back to the 15th century. Admire the architectural gems like the Church of the Assumption of Mary, boasting a Gothic-Renaissance style, and the Duke's Palace, a testament to Pag's former status as a ducal capital.

Immerse yourself in the exquisite art of Pag lace, a UNESCO-protected intangible cultural heritage. This intricate needlework, passed down through generations, features delicate patterns and motifs that reflect the town's rich heritage. Visit the Lacemaking Museum to learn about the history and techniques of this unique craft, or admire the impressive collection of lace pieces at the Benedictine Convent.

Escape the hustle and bustle and bask in the tranquility of Prosika Beach, the largest sandy beach near the old town. For a more adventurous experience, venture into the Pag bay and discover its 27 kilometers of diverse beaches, ranging from soft sand to smooth pebbles and rugged rocks.

Whether you're into history, culture, chilling on the beach, or exploring nature, Pag has something for everyone. Come experience the magic of Pag this summer! It's the perfect place to make unforgettable memories with friends and family.
